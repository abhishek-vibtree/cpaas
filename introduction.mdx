## What OneML is

**OneML** is OneInbox's own programmable-voice platform. It is a **strict superset of TwiML**: the markup on the wire is TwiML (same `<Response>` root, same verbs, same attributes, same webhook parameter names), branded OneML. An application written for Twilio Programmable Voice runs against OneML by pointing its webhook/API domain at OneML — no dialect translation.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Same webhook contract" icon="webhook">
    Your number's Voice URL returns TwiML/OneML. OneML fetches it, executes the verbs against a real media engine, and posts the same callbacks Twilio does.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Same REST shape" icon="server">
    Resources live under <code>/2010-04-01/Accounts/&#123;AccountSid&#125;/…</code> with HTTP Basic auth — a drop-in for <code>api.twilio.com</code>.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Same signature" icon="shield-check">
    Every outbound webhook is signed with <code>X-Twilio-Signature</code> (HMAC-SHA1), byte-identical to Twilio's algorithm, plus an <code>X-OneML-Signature</code> alias.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Runs on your carrier" icon="phone">
    Calls terminate on your own Asterisk/PJSIP trunks and DID inventory (Japan today), routed by your dashboard's Routes table.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What "change the domain" means (and doesn't)

<Note>
The drop-in promise holds for the **HTTP contract** — TwiML webhooks and the REST resource shape. It does **not** cover the official Twilio SDKs (they hardcode `api.twilio.com`; use a base-URL override or raw HTTP), nor the global carrier network/number inventory (OneML is regional).
</Note>

## Where OneML is today

OneML already runs, live on real calls, a large fraction of the Twilio voice contract:

- **Verbs:** `<Play>` (local audio), `<Gather>` (DTMF), `<Dial>` with `<Number>`, `<Sip>`, and `<Conference>` nouns, `<Hangup>`, `<Reject>`, `<Redirect>`.
- **Continuations:** `<Dial action>` and `<Gather action>` callbacks (DialCallStatus / Digits).
- **Status callbacks:** call-progress (`initiated`/`ringing`/`answered`/`completed`), signed.
- **Conference:** the full `<Conference>` verb plus the Participants REST API (add, list, mute, hold, kick, end) and every conference status event.
- **Outbound routing:** REST-originated calls resolve through your dashboard Routes table (trunk + number/CLI formatting).

For a precise implemented-vs-pending matrix, see [Compatibility & roadmap](/compatibility).

<Warning>
Some Twilio features are **not yet implemented**: `<Client>`/WebRTC (browser agents), `<Record>` + recordings, `<Say>` TTS, `<Play>` of remote audio URLs, SMS (`/Messages.json`), and REST `CreateCall` (`/Calls.json`). These are tracked on the [Compatibility](/compatibility) page.
</Warning>
