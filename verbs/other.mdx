## `<Hangup>`

Ends the call. Terminal.

```xml
<Response>
  <Play>goodbye</Play>
  <Hangup/>
</Response>
```

## `<Reject>`

Declines the call. OneML answers and plays a "no service" announcement, then hangs up. A rejected call is reported as **not answered**.

```xml
<Response>
  <Reject reason="busy"/>
</Response>
```

<ParamField path="reason" type="string" default="rejected">Parsed. `busy` or `rejected`.</ParamField>

## `<Redirect>`

Transfers control of the call to a new OneML document at another URL. OneML fetches it (signed) and runs it, replacing the current program.

```xml
<Response>
  <Redirect method="POST">https://your-app.example/next-step</Redirect>
</Response>
```

<ParamField path="method" type="string" default="POST">HTTP method for the fetch.</ParamField>

## `<Say>` — not yet executed

<Warning>
`<Say>` is **parsed and accepted but skipped** — there is no text-to-speech engine yet. Use `<Play>` with built-in audio for prompts. TTS is on the [roadmap](/compatibility).
</Warning>

```xml
<!-- accepted, but currently produces no audio -->
<Response>
  <Say>Hello and welcome</Say>
</Response>
```

Attributes `voice`, `language`, and `loop` are parsed for forward compatibility.

## `<Pause>` — not yet executed

<Warning>
`<Pause>` is parsed and accepted but currently **skipped** (no silent wait yet).
</Warning>

<ParamField path="length" type="integer" default="1">Parsed. Seconds to pause.</ParamField>
